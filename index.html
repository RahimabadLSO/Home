Who We Are

Building Stronger Communities Together

Rahimabad Local Support Organization® (RLSO) is a registered non-for-profit organization established to serve the communities of Union Council Rahimabad. Since its founding, RLSO has been committed to inclusive community development that leaves no one behind.

We operate through a network of Village Organizations, Women Organizations, Youth Organizations, Community-Based Savings Groups, and Community Organizations — bringing together thousands of community members toward shared goals of prosperity and well-being.

Our programs span education, livelihood support, health awareness, women empowerment, and youth development — creating lasting, transformative change across the region.

Our Mission

To foster sustainable development, social equity, and economic empowerment within Union Council Rahimabad by mobilizing communities, building local organizations, and delivering high-impact programs that address the root causes of poverty and marginalization.

01
Community Mobilization

Organizing villages into structured groups for collective action and shared decision-making.

02
Women Empowerment

Enabling women to lead, save, and participate meaningfully in community development.

03
Youth Development

Building capacity and creating opportunities for the next generation of community leaders.

04
Savings & Microfinance

Facilitating community-based savings groups to build financial resilience at the grassroots.

05
Infrastructure Development

Improving access to clean water, sanitation, roads, and essential community facilities.

RLSO was created to be that platform. A space where ordinary people could come together, organize, and take charge of their own future. I am humbled by how far we have come, and I trust that the seeds we planted will continue to grow for generations."

Our Legacy

Completed Projects & Activities

A record of transformative work RLSO has accomplished since its founding — milestones that define our journey.

2012–2013 · Foundation Years

Community Mobilization & Organization Formation Drive

Conducted the foundational community mobilization campaign across all villages in Union Council Rahimabad, forming the first wave of Village Organizations, Women Organizations, and Community Organizations. Trained community leaders and conducted awareness sessions on collective action, savings, and participatory development planning.

2013–2014 · Infrastructure Phase

Rural Water Supply & Sanitation Project

Constructed 25 water hand-pumps and 40 household latrines across 6 villages, dramatically reducing waterborne diseases and improving hygiene standards. The project was a community-led initiative with households contributing labor and RLSO managing procurement and technical oversight.

2014–2015 · Livelihoods Phase

Women's Livelihood Support Program — Phase I

Established 3 vocational training centers providing sewing, knitting, and food processing skills to over 150 women. Provided small startup kits to 80 graduates, enabling them to start home-based enterprises. Program led to a measurable increase in household income among beneficiaries.

2015–2016 · Education Initiative

Literacy Campaign & Adult Education Program

Ran literacy classes for over 200 adults (primarily women) across 5 villages in partnership with local schools. Distributed free learning materials and trained volunteer teachers. The campaign achieved an 85% literacy completion rate among enrolled participants.

2016–2018 · Savings & Finance

Community-Based Savings Groups Launch

Established the first cohort of Community-Based Savings Groups (CBSGs) across Rahimabad. By end of 2018, over 10 CBSGs were actively operating with a combined savings pool exceeding PKR 2.5 million. Provided microloans to over 120 families for agriculture, livestock, and small businesses.

Youth Development & Sports Tournament

COVID-19 Community Relief & Awareness Drive

2022–2023 · Flood Relief

2022 Flood Rehabilitation Program

In the wake of catastrophic 2022 floods, RLSO led a rapid relief and rehabilitation effort. Over 300 flood-affected families received emergency food, tents, and household supplies. RLSO coordinated with government agencies and NGOs to rebuild 45 damaged homes and restore 3 community water points.
